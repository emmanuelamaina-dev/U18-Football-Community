ANSWERS & GUIDES

Frequently asked questions

Choose a topic or search for a question.

12 questions
How do I create an account?

Choose Sign up and complete the form with your username, email, full name, football position, and password. Your password must be at least eight characters, and you’ll need to enter it twice. After registration, go to the login page to sign in.

What should I do if I can’t log in?

Check that you’re using the username or email and password associated with your account, and make sure there are no extra spaces. If you still can’t sign in, ask a parent or guardian to help you contact the person who manages this website. Don’t send your password in a message.

How do I edit my profile?

Sign in, open Profile, and select Edit Profile. You can update your player name, bio, and position. Keep personal details in your bio to a minimum, especially information that could identify where you live or go to school.

If I delete my account, what happens to my user data?

This website does not currently offer a self-service account deletion option, so there is no automatic deletion process to describe. Ask the site administrator or a trusted adult to contact the person managing the website and request account removal and information about what will happen to your profile, goals, and other records. Don’t include your password in the request.

How can I track my football progress?

Open your player profile to add goals and view your goals, achievements, challenges, and progress sections. The Progress page also provides access to progress information. Your profile is tied to your signed-in account.

How do I add or update a goal?

Sign in and open your profile. In the Goals section, choose the option to add a goal, pick a timeframe (weekly, monthly, or yearly), enter what you want to work on, and save. Return to the goal later to update or remove it.

What are team challenges?

Challenges are activities designed to help teammates build skills such as teamwork, communication, resilience, and support. Visit Challenges to browse the activities and their instructions.

How do I recognise or encourage a teammate?

Visit Lift Others Up to explore the community encouragement features. Keep messages respectful, specific, and supportive, and never use them to share someone else’s private information.

As a parent, how can I track my child’s progress?

Progress is shown in the player’s profile and progress areas after the player signs in. This site does not currently provide a separate parent dashboard or parent login for tracking a child. Ask your child to show you their progress if they are comfortable, and discuss goals together without asking them to share their password.

Can I create an account for my child?

A parent or guardian should help the young player review the site’s community rules and privacy information before signing up. Use accurate details and an email address the family can access. A parent should not impersonate the child or ask them to share a password.

Is it possible for someone to hack into my account?

No online service can promise that an account is impossible to compromise. This site stores password hashes rather than plain-text passwords, but you should still use a long, unique password, keep it private, sign out on shared devices, and avoid suspicious links. Tell a parent or guardian and the site administrator if you notice activity you don’t recognize.

What personal information should I share?

Only provide information needed to use the site. Don’t post your password, home address, phone number, school timetable, or live location. Ask a parent or guardian before sharing a photo or any detail you’re unsure about, and read the site rules and privacy information.
