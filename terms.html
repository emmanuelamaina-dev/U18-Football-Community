WELCOME

Welcome to the U18 Football Community

The U18 Football Community is designed to provide a positive, supportive and inclusive digital community for young football players and the adults who support them.

The platform allows members to celebrate achievements, recognise positive contributions, track progress and participate in community challenges.

02
OUR PLEDGE

The Community Pledge

“

I pledge to use the U18 Football Community responsibly, respectfully and safely. I will treat other members with dignity, protect my own privacy and respect the privacy of others.

I will use the platform to encourage, recognise and support others rather than to embarrass, insult or harm them.

I understand that my actions affect other members of the community, and I accept responsibility for using this platform appropriately.

03
RESPECT

Respectful Behaviour

Every member is expected to treat other users with respect and dignity.

✓ DO

  • Communicate politely.
  • Encourage teammates.
  • Respect different abilities.
  • Accept constructive feedback.
  • Celebrate improvement.
  • Use appropriate language.

✕ DO NOT

  • Bully or harass another member.
  • Make threats.
  • Humiliate another player.
  • Spread harmful rumours.
  • Discriminate against others.
  • Target another member.
04
SAFETY

Safety & Safeguarding

The safety and wellbeing of young people is a priority of the U18 Football Community.

!

Keep yourself and others safe

Do not use the platform to arrange unsafe meetings, pressure someone into meeting privately, request inappropriate personal information, threaten another member or encourage unsafe behaviour.

If something happening on the platform makes you feel unsafe or uncomfortable, speak to a trusted adult and report the concern through the appropriate channel.

05
PRIVACY

Privacy & Personal Information

We believe that users should understand what information they provide and how it is used.

Information that may be collected

01 Name
02 Username
03 Email address
04 Community role
05 Football position
06 Goals and milestones
07 Recognition information
08 Progress information

Keep private information private

Users should never publicly share passwords, home addresses, financial information, real-time locations or another person's private information.

06
ACCOUNT SECURITY

Accounts & Access

Users are responsible for keeping their account secure.

✓

Keep your password private.

✓

Log out when using a shared device.

✓

Report suspicious account activity.

✕

Do not access another person's account.

✕

Do not attempt to bypass security controls.

Role-Based Access

Different users may have different permissions. Players, coaches, guardians and administrators should only access information and features appropriate to their authorised role.

07
RECOGNITION

Lift Others Up

Recognition features exist to celebrate positive contributions and genuine effort.

MOST IMPROVED Celebrate development.
MOST PERSISTENT Celebrate determination.
MOST SUPPORTIVE Celebrate encouragement.
BEST TEAMMATE Celebrate teamwork.

Recognition must never be used to embarrass, shame or deliberately target another player.

08
SPEAK UP

Reporting Concerns

Members should report behaviour that makes them or another person feel unsafe.

01 Bullying
02 Harassment
03 Threats
04 Privacy concerns
05 Suspicious accounts
06 Inappropriate content
✓

No retaliation

Users must not target, threaten or punish someone because they made a genuine report.

09
CONTENT

Content Rules

Content submitted to the platform should be respectful, relevant, appropriate and encouraging.

✓ Respectful messages
✓ Genuine recognition
✓ Constructive feedback
✕ Bullying or harassment
✕ Threats
✕ Private information
10
ACCOUNT ACTION

Account Suspension

An account may be restricted, suspended or removed if a user repeatedly or seriously violates the community rules.

  • Repeated bullying or harassment.
  • Unauthorised access attempts.
  • Misuse of personal information.
  • Impersonation.
  • Harmful or prohibited content.
  • Deliberate misuse of community features.
✓
FINAL AGREEMENT

Are you ready to be part of the community?

By creating an account, you agree to respect the people, privacy and principles of the U18 Football Community.
